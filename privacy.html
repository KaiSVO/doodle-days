Doodle Days Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Doodle Days is a calendar you can draw in. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your entries stay on your device

Everything you create in Doodle Days (doodles, notes, plans, trackers and photos) is stored on your own device. We don't run any servers that receive your entries, and we can't see them.

Accounts are optional

You can use every part of Doodle Days without an account. If you want your entries on more than one device, you can choose to sign in with your Google account. Signing in is coming in a future update; this section explains how it will work.

  • What we ask Google for. Your basic profile (name, email address and picture), so the app can show which account is connected, and access to a private app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission).
  • Where synced data goes. The app saves a copy of your entries in that private folder, in your own Google Drive, and reads it on your other devices. The folder is hidden from your regular Drive files, and Doodle Days can't see or change anything else in your Drive.
  • What we receive. Nothing. Your data travels directly between your device and Google. We don't keep a copy and don't run a server for accounts.
  • Signing out and deleting. You can sign out at any time in Settings. Signing out leaves your entries on the device. To delete the synced copy, use the delete option in Settings, or remove Doodle Days' access in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps and services.
  • Google Calendar. If you later choose to show your Google Calendar events in Doodle Days, the app asks for read-only access. Events are shown on your device only and are never changed, stored on a server or shared.

Doodle Days' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.

No ads or tracking

  • The app shows no ads.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we don't sell or share personal data.

Photos

If you add a photo to a day, the app makes a smaller copy and saves it on your device with your other entries. Photos are never uploaded anywhere, except into your own Google Drive app folder if you turn on sync.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses them only to show reminders for plans you created.

Purchases

The optional Supporter purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We don't receive your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

Fonts

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, including any font you pick in Settings, which means your device contacts Google's servers to download them. See Google's privacy policy for how they handle these requests.

Backups

When you save a backup file, it's saved to your device. Where you keep or share that file is up to you.

Children

Doodle Days doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
