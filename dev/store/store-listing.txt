Full description (4000 characters max)
Doodle Days is a calendar you can draw in. Every day gets its own little page for a doodle, a note, a photo and the things you made. It's made for artists, crafters and anyone who likes to keep track of life with a pen instead of a spreadsheet.

DRAW ON EVERY DAY
• Doodle with pen, marker and eraser. Lines get thicker when you press harder with a stylus.
• Add stickers from the sticker tray, or tap a recent one.
• Undo with a two-finger tap, redo with three.
• Notes tab: a notepad where you can type and doodle on the same page, with headings, lists and checklists.

PLAN YOUR MAKING
• Add plans for classes, markets, fairs and deadlines, with repeats and reminders.
• Month, week and year views.
• Colour a day to mark it, like green for a day off.
• Search finds notes, plans and activities, and matching days glow on the calendar.
• Share my calendar: send a picture of your month or week, or send your plans so friends can add them to their own calendar.

BUILD LITTLE STREAKS
• Track the things you make, like painting, knitting or shop orders.
• Give each activity a colour and a little icon, then tap "Mark today done" to grow a streak. One missed day a week is forgiven.
• Set a monthly goal and see how close you are.
• At the end of each month, get a look back at everything you made.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Themes, fonts and sticker finishes.
• Light and dark mode.
• Available in English, Ukrainian, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French and Polish.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your entries stay on your device. No account needed, no ads, no tracking.
• Lock the app with a PIN or your fingerprint.
• Save a backup file to move to a new phone.
• Deleted something by mistake? It stays in Recently deleted for 30 days.
